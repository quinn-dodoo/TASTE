04 — CONCEPT & CALL TO ACTION

Chuck Palahniuk’s statement, “The things you own end up owning you,” exposes how thin the line is between mastering material possessions and becoming enslaved by them. This idea aligns with Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production, highlighting that overconsumption is often driven not by actual needs, but by the desire to construct and define our identities. When our identity becomes tied to what we buy, we fall into an endless cycle of consumption to constantly "upgrade" our image, silently draining both global resources and personal freedom. This project focuses on exploring that hidden reality: how material objects gradually expand, take over our space, and consume our true selves.

CALL TO ACTION

Recognise the pattern. Question the consumption. Redefine your worth beyond material possessions.
